Newton’s Law of Cooling
Here is a slight variation of an exponential model. This says that the rate at which a body cools
is proportional to the difference between the temperature of the body and the temperature of
the surrounding medium. That is,
T ′(t) = k(T − Tm),
where T (t) is the temperature of the body at time t, k is the constant of proportionality and Tm
is the temperature of the surrounding medium.
